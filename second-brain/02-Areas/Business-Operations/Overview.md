# Business Operations — Overview

Practice management, staff, equipment, and administrative operations for Gulf Coast Limb and Brace.

---

## Areas

### Credentialing & Compliance
- ABC credentialing maintenance (CPO renewal cycle, CE requirements)
- DMEPOS supplier enrollment (Medicare, Medicaid, commercial payers)
- State licensure (O&P license renewal)
- HIPAA compliance (BAAs with vendors, staff training)
- Accreditation (BOC or ACHC accreditation for Medicare billing)

### Equipment & Facility
- Fabrication lab equipment maintenance schedule
- Supply vendor contacts
- Component inventory (feet, knees, liners, sockets)

### Administrative
- Intake and referral workflow
- Patient scheduling
- Prior auth tracking
- Claims status follow-up
