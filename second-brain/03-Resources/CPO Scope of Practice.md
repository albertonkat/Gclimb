# CPO Scope of Practice

Alberto Alvarez, CPO — Gulf Coast Limb and Brace

---

## Credentials

**CPO — Certified Prosthetist Orthotist**  
Credentialing body: ABC (American Board for Certification in Orthotics, Prosthetics and Pedorthics)  
Website: abcop.org

ABC CPO credential covers the full scope of both prosthetist and orthotist practice.

---

## Scope of Practice

A CPO is qualified to:
- Evaluate, design, fabricate, fit, and deliver custom prostheses and orthoses
- Perform patient evaluations including functional assessments (K-level)
- Prescribe and document medical necessity for O&P devices
- Provide patient education and gait training
- Fabricate and modify devices in a clinical lab setting
- Bill Medicare and other payers for O&P services under their NPI

A CPO **cannot** (without additional credentials):
- Prescribe medications
- Perform surgery
- Order imaging independently (must be ordered by physician/mid-level)
- Provide physical therapy (separate PT credential required)

## ABC Certification Maintenance

### Continuing Education
- **75 CE credits required per 5-year renewal cycle**
- At least 1 credit in ethics
- Credits can be earned through ABC-approved courses, AOPA, AOPA Academy, state associations

### ABC Renewal
- **Renewal cycle:** Every 5 years from initial certification date
- Late renewal penalty applies after grace period
- Lapse in certification affects Medicare billing eligibility

### State Licensure
Many states require a separate O&P state license in addition to ABC certification. Requirements vary.

## Medicare Enrollment

To bill Medicare, a CPO must be:
1. **Enrolled as a Medicare supplier** (DMEPOS supplier enrollment through PECOS)
2. **Accredited** through BOC or ACHC (required for DMEPOS suppliers)
3. **Surety bond** in place (required for DMEPOS enrollment)
4. **NPI** active and linked to billing entity
