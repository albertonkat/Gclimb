# Insurance Payers — O&P Reference

Payer landscape, prior auth requirements, contacts, and learned denial patterns for Gulf Coast Limb and Brace.

---

## Medicare

**Carrier type:** MAC (Medicare Administrative Contractor) — region-specific  
**Claims submission:** Electronic 837P (preferred) or paper CMS-1500  
**Timely filing:** 12 months from date of service  
**Appeals:** 5 levels (Redetermination → Reconsideration → ALJ → MAC → Federal Court)

### Prior Authorization Program

CMS requires prior auth for certain DMEPOS items in select states and nationwide for high-cost prosthetics:
- **Microprocessor-controlled knees** (L5856, L5857, L5858, L5973, L7362, L7364, L7367)
- **Lower limb prostheses above a certain cost threshold** — check current CMS guidance

Prior auth request must include: clinical notes, K-level documentation, device specification, physician order.

**Authorization does not guarantee payment** — claims still reviewed for billing accuracy.

### Key Medicare Billing Rules
- **KX modifier**: Attests that documentation in file supports LCD coverage criteria — required for covered claims
- **ABN required** when coverage is uncertain and patient chooses to pay
- **Competitive Bidding**: Check if your area is a competitive bidding area (CBA) — affects reimbursement rates and supplier enrollment requirements
- **Assignment**: Must accept assignment for Medicare beneficiaries (accept Medicare-approved amount as payment in full)

## Medicaid

**Varies by state** — each state has its own fee schedule and prior auth requirements.

### Common Rules
- Prior auth almost always required for prosthetics and custom orthotics
- Lower fee schedules than Medicare in most states
- Some states carve O&P into managed care plans — verify coverage by plan
- Replacement rules often stricter than Medicare

## Blue Cross Blue Shield

**Multiple local plans** — verify which BCBS entity covers the patient (national vs. local, FEP vs. commercial).

| Plan type | Prior auth threshold |
|---|---|
| Commercial individual/group | Usually required for prosthetics >$500; custom orthotics |
| FEP (Federal Employee Program) | Generally follows Medicare LCD; prior auth required for prosthetics |
| BCBS Medicare Advantage | Follows Medicare criteria; prior auth may still be required |

### Tips
- Check the patient's card for the BCBS subsidiary — policies differ significantly
- FEP plans are typically more generous than commercial BCBS
- BCBS often uses InterQual or MCG clinical criteria — document against these standards

---

## UnitedHealthcare (UHC)

**Prior auth:** Required for most prosthetics and custom orthotics  
**Auth portal:** UHC Provider Portal (myuhc.com/provider)  
**Clinical criteria:** UHC uses its own coverage policies — search at uhcprovider.com  
**Timely filing:** 90–180 days depending on plan type

### Common UHC Patterns
- Frequently requests additional clinical documentation after initial auth request
- Peer-to-peer review available when denied — request within appeal window
- Medicare Advantage UHC plans generally follow Medicare LCD but may add restrictions

---

## Aetna

**Prior auth:** Required for prosthetics and custom AFO/KAFO  
**Auth portal:** Aetna Navigator  
**Clinical criteria:** Aetna Clinical Policy Bulletins (CPBs) — available at aetna.com  
**Timely filing:** 180 days

### Aetna CPB Notes for O&P
- CPB 0567: Lower limb prosthetics — closely mirrors Medicare K-level criteria
- CPB 0095: Orthotics — requires documented diagnosis and functional need
- Microprocessor knees: covered for K3/K4 with specific documentation

---

## Cigna

**Prior auth:** Required for prosthetics over threshold; custom orthotics  
**Auth portal:** CignaForHCP.com  
**Clinical criteria:** Cigna Coverage Policies (medical necessity criteria)  
**Timely filing:** 180 days

### Cigna Notes
- Follows similar K-level framework to Medicare for prosthetics
- Often requires functional outcome data for microprocessor devices
- Commercial vs. Cigna Medicare Advantage — different criteria

---

## VA / CHAMPVA

**VA Direct Care:** O&P services provided at VA facilities OR by community providers through VA Community Care Network (CCN)  
**VA CCN:** Administered by Optum for most regions — prior auth required via VA referral  
**CHAMPVA:** For dependents/survivors of 100% disabled veterans — coverage is generous, closely follows Medicare  

### VA Tips
- Authorization comes as a VA consult/referral — bill to the CCN administrator (Optum)
- No out-of-pocket for veterans in VA direct care
- Microprocessor knees and advanced componentry often covered for K2 veterans (VA is more generous than Medicare)
- Document veteran's activity level, vocational demands, and goals carefully

---

## Tricare

**Administered by:** Defense Health Agency  
**Plans:** Tricare Prime, Tricare Select, Tricare for Life (TFL — secondary to Medicare)  
**Prior auth:** Required for most prosthetics; submit through Humana Military (East) or WPS Military (West)  
**Timely filing:** 1 year from date of service  
**Coverage:** Generous — often follows VA standards for active duty

---

## Workers' Compensation

**Carrier varies by employer / state**  
**Billing rules:** Dictated by individual state WC fee schedules  
**Prior auth:** Almost always required; submit to WC adjuster/case manager  
**Key documentation:** Causation must be clearly linked to work injury  
**Timely filing:** Varies — often shorter windows; confirm with adjuster

### Tips
- Get case manager name and phone; direct communication usually speeds auth
- Some WC carriers use IME (independent medical exam) to challenge necessity
- Document functional limitations directly related to the work injury
