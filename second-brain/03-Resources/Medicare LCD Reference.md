# Medicare LCD Reference — O&P

Local Coverage Determinations (LCDs) issued by Medicare Administrative Contractors (MACs) govern what O&P devices Medicare Part B covers and what documentation is required.

---

## Key LCDs for O&P

| LCD | Title | Covers |
|---|---|---|
| **L33686** | Lower Limb Prostheses | BK, AK, hip disarticulation prostheses; K-level requirements |
| **L33679** | Lower Limb Orthotics | AFO, KAFO, KO; medically necessary criteria |
| **L33697** | Spinal Orthotics | Cervical, LSO, TLSO; diagnosis and documentation requirements |
| **L33657** | Ankle-Foot/Knee-Ankle-Foot Orthosis | AFO/KAFO custom vs. prefab; off-the-shelf criteria |
| **L33791** | Orthopedic Footwear and Therapeutic Shoe Inserts | Diabetic shoe program (A-codes) |

> LCD numbers vary by MAC. Verify with your region's MAC (Noridian, NGS, CGS, Palmetto GBA, WPS, First Coast).

## Lower Limb Prosthetics (L33686) — Key Rules

### K-level Coverage Criteria

| K-level | Medicare Will Cover |
|---|---|
| K0 | No prosthesis covered |
| K1 | Basic lower limb prosthesis — SACH foot, single axis constant friction knee |
| K2 | Basic lower limb — limited dynamic response components |
| K3 | Dynamic response feet, fluid-controlled knees (up to microprocessor) |
| K4 | High-performance components; microprocessor knee generally covered |

**K0 and K1 only**: Simple SACH foot, no microprocessor components.  
**K2**: Dynamic response foot possible; single-axis knee (AK).  
**K3**: Carbon fiber dynamic response feet, fluid-controlled knee mechanisms.  
**K4**: High-performance energy return feet, microprocessor knees (L5857).

### Documentation Requirements for Lower Limb Prosthetics

All of the following must be in the record:

- [ ] **Physician order** — signed, dated, includes diagnosis and specific device
- [ ] **CPO evaluation note** — face-to-face, documents residual limb condition, functional assessment
- [ ] **K-level determination** — stated level + clinical rationale (not just the number)
- [ ] **ICD-10 diagnosis code(s)** — amputation code (Z89.xxx) + etiology
- [ ] **Functional prognosis** — ability to benefit from the device prescribed
- [ ] **Device specification** — HCPCS codes for all components being billed
- [ ] **Delivery documentation** — date of delivery, condition of device, patient signature
- [ ] **ABN (if applicable)** — when prescribing upgrade above covered K-level

### Microprocessor Knee (L5857) — Special Requirements

- Patient must be **K3 or K4**
- Patient must have been **ambulatory with a prosthesis** or have high likelihood of achieving K3 function
- **Prior authorization** required under the prior auth program for certain MACs
- Documentation must include: activity level, fall history, daily ambulation distance, vocational demands

### Replacement Criteria

Replacement of a prosthesis is covered when:
- **Change in the patient's condition** that alters the fit or function
- **Normal wear and tear** after typical useful lifetime (usually 3–5 years)
- **Loss or irreparable damage** beyond repair

Document: why replacement is needed, current device condition, patient's continued ambulatory status.

## AFO/KAFO (L33657 / L33679) — Key Rules

### Custom vs. Prefabricated

**Custom fabricated** (higher reimbursement) requires:
- Unique patient characteristics that make a prefab device inadequate
- Documentation of why off-the-shelf does not meet needs
- Fabricated from a model or cast of the patient

**Prefabricated** (prefab): for conditions where standard sizing is appropriate. Less documentation required but must still justify medical necessity.

**Off-the-shelf (OTS)**: Minimal documentation. Patient can self-apply and adjust. Used for mild conditions.

### Medical Necessity for AFO

Must document:
- Neurological or musculoskeletal diagnosis causing gait dysfunction
- Specific functional limitation the AFO addresses (e.g., foot drop, ankle instability)
- Failure of conservative treatment when applicable
- That the patient has sufficient upper body strength and cognitive ability to use the device safely

### KAFO Additional Requirements

- More stringent — must document why AFO alone is inadequate
- Knee instability or weakness that requires external stabilization at the knee joint
- Patient must have the functional ability to use a KAFO (hip flexion, transfer ability)

## Spinal Orthotics (L33697) — Key Rules

- **Post-surgical**: Coverage generally clear with surgical documentation
- **Conservative treatment for pain**: Requires documentation of failed conservative measures (PT, NSAIDs, injections)
- **Fracture**: ICD-10 fracture code with imaging documentation
- **Scoliosis**: Cobb angle measurement, patient age, active growth phase (skeletally immature)
- **Rigid vs. flexible**: Rigid custom TLSO requires documented need for rigid control; flexible LSO has lower threshold

## Common Denial Reasons and LCD-Based Responses

| Denial | LCD-Based Response |
|---|---|
| "Not medically necessary — K-level not supported" | Submit CPO evaluation with K-level rationale; functional assessment (TUG, ambulation distance, vocational demands); physician attestation |
| "Missing face-to-face evaluation" | Submit signed/dated CPO note; ensure it documents the exam components specified in LCD |
| "Custom fabrication not justified" | Submit measurements/cast documentation; statement why prefab inadequate |
| "Replacement too frequent" | Document clinical reason (weight change, volume change, skin breakdown); photos if available |
| "Microprocessor knee not covered — K-level" | K3/K4 functional assessment documentation; fall history; ambulation goals |

---

## ABN Situations

Issue an ABN when:
- Prescribing K3/K4 componentry for a patient whose documented function supports only K1/K2
- Patient requests upgrade beyond Medicare coverage level
- Device is likely to be denied for frequency limitation
- Billing for a device where LCD coverage is uncertain
